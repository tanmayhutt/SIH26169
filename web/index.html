Architecture and delivery readiness

Service boundaries, state, failure behaviour and the next gate for every major subsystem. Percentages are weighted readiness gates (done counts 1, in progress 0.5), not estimates of time remaining.

Green marks a satisfied gate, amber partial work, red something blocked by a physical or model limit, and faint grey a gate nobody has started. Architecture percentages are weighted readiness gates, not delivery dates. Inventory items tagged with a PS row implement that row of the problem statement. The dated verification is a snapshot of runs in the repository's results folder; other statuses are maintained in web/progress.json.
